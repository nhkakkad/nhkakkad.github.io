About the Doctor

Your Doctor,
Your Ally

"Mental illness is not a personal failing. My goal is to make every patient feel safe, understood, and hopeful about their path forward."

Dr. Nidhish Kakkad is a consultant psychiatrist based in Junagadh, Gujarat, specialising in diagnosing and treating a wide spectrum of mental health conditions. He combines the latest evidence-based treatments with a deeply human, patient-first approach that puts dignity at the centre of every consultation.
